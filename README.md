komorebi 🌤️

A single-file, no-build pomodoro timer and monthly habit tracker with two moods: a soft Ghibli-inspired dusk scene, and a dark retro-space theme. Drop in a YouTube or Spotify link to study to.

Live site: add your link here after deploying, e.g. https://kaushik-negi.github.io/komorebi/

Features

Timer

Pomodoro / short break / long break modes
Adjustable durations, auto-start, and completion chime — all configurable in settings
HH:MM:SS display with a sun/moon that travels across the sky as the session progresses
Session-round dots and keyboard shortcut (space to start/pause)

Habit tracker

Dashboard grid — add goals, tick/cross each day of the month, see monthly % per goal and daily totals
Calendar view — month at a glance, shaded by how much of the day's goals were completed
Heatmap — GitHub-style yearly contribution graph, navigate between years
Stats: current streak, best streak, this month %, total check-ins

Theme toggle: Ghibli dusk ⇄ dark retro-space (synthwave sun, scanlines, neon grid)
Optional YouTube / Spotify embed for background music
Everything is saved locally in your browser (localStorage) — no account, no backend, no data leaves your device
